served from `/opt/infiniteocean/site` on the Infinite Ocean VPS. They keep the
currently live company site aligned with the canonical Vue policy routes while
the production Spyglass application is not yet deployed.

Deploy only the two versioned directories, preserve the server-owned site
shell and assets, and verify the public HTTPS bodies after replacement.
