About Infinite Ocean

Software for people who are doing more with less.

Our goal is simple: give small businesses access to the kind of operational capability that normally requires a larger team.

What we build

We build tools that can research, organize, follow up, monitor, plan and carry work forward—not just answer questions.

Spyglass is our business platform built around that idea. It gives business owners a team of AI agents that can take on real work, keep track of what needs to happen next and help prevent important things from falling through the cracks.

What we believe

AI is most useful when it disappears into the work.

  • It should save time.
  • It should help you respond faster.
  • It should give you capabilities you did not have before.
  • At the end of the day, something should actually be done.
