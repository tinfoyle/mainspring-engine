About Infinite Ocean

Small company. Serious product.

Infinite Ocean, LLC is an independent software company building practical tools for businesses that have more work than time.

Why Infinite Ocean exists

Most small businesses do not need more dashboards, jargon or complicated systems. They need a clear picture of the work, a reliable way to keep it moving and fewer loose ends living in somebody’s head.

We build software around that reality. The goal is not to make a business look more technical. The goal is to make the day easier to run.

What we are building

Our first commercial product is Spyglass. It brings jobs, customers, schedules, knowledge and carefully limited AI helpers together, then puts the questions and approvals that need a person into one place: Your Turn.

Who is behind it

Infinite Ocean is founded and operated by Benjamin Pace, a software engineer in North Carolina. The company is deliberately small, independent and focused on building a product people can understand and trust.
