01

Get the business in one place.

Bring together the customers, jobs, schedules, notes and working knowledge your team already has.

  • Keep the facts with the work they belong to.
  • Make missing information easier to spot.
  • Give the whole team the same picture.
02

Let routine work move.

Your team and AI helpers can take on clear responsibilities with the tools, schedules and limits you set.

  • Give helpers actual jobs instead of vague access.
  • Keep a record of what happened.
  • Stop and ask when judgment is required.
03

Open Your Turn.

Questions, reviews and approvals land in one focused list. Each item tells you what happened, what it needs and what follows your answer.

  • See the decisions only you can make.
  • Deal with the important item before the noisy one.
  • Move on knowing the rest is still being tracked.
